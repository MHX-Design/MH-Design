Black Moss Shell

Black Moss Shell is a third-person stealth-action concept set in a decaying, post-industrial world where nature has reclaimed much of humanity's technology. My focus was on designing the pre-game customisation menu for characters, weapons, and equipment, combining UI/UX, motion, and visual design.

The interface reflects the game’s tone through minimal, intuitive UX, with motion, sound, and visual design working together to enhance immersion with clear, tactical decision-making before gameplay begins.

Role: UI/UX · Motion Design · Visual Design · Interaction Design

Tools used: Figma, After Effects, Photoshop, Illustrator, AI-Assisted Tools*
(*character, weapon, equipment, audio, and supporting copy ideation)

User Flow & System Overview

I conducted research into common customer pain points, which informed a detailed storyboard outlining each step of the setup process.
